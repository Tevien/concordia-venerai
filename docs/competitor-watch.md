## 2026-07-31 - HereAfter AI is SHUTTING DOWN

The consumer flagship of the category (voice-interviewer → clip-playback
"Legacy Avatar", $3.99–7.99/mo) announced closure on its own homepage; users
must email support to retrieve their life-story recordings.

**Sector scoreboard now:** StoryFile — Chapter 11 (continuing to operate).
HereAfter — shut down. The two best-known retrieval-based consumer products
have both failed commercially.

**Two honest readings, both true:**
1. *Validation:* demand exists (HereAfter had years of users and press) but
   clip-playback was too shallow to retain families — you can't ask it
   anything. Grounded-generative is the depth the category needed.
2. *Warning:* consumer legacy-tech is a hard business — low willingness to
   pay, delicate grief-adjacent marketing, slow purchase cycles. Their $4–8/mo
   pricing failing is not proof our $10 works; our margin case rests on the
   daily-moment product being *worth more*, not on optimism.

**The strategic lesson — abandonment is the sector's second failure mode**
(after trust scandal): families gave HereAfter their parents' voices and now
race a shutdown clock to get them back. Every prospective customer will soon
ask "what happens to the twin when *your* startup dies?" We should answer
structurally, not reassuringly: an explicit continuity & portability promise
(everything exportable, open formats, guaranteed wind-down export window).
Cheap to promise honestly — our data model is genuinely exportable (S3 media +
transcripts + persona/memory JSON).

**Opportunity:** displaced HereAfter families hold exactly what our Weave
ingests (audio stories, photos). "Bring your recordings" import is already
functionally supported (artifact uploads); it's a positioning/landing-page
angle, not engineering.

## 2026-07-16 - Landscape map (from the USP-verification sweep)

- **HereAfter AI** — retrieval clip-playback avatar, $3.99–7.99/mo. (Now dead;
  see above.)
- **StoryFile** — recorded-video Q&A retrieval; Chapter 11, still operating.
- **DeepBrain Re;memory** — generative studio avatars of the deceased,
  $10k–100k creation + ~$1,200/conversation; memorial showrooms (KR).
- **Eternos / You, Only Virtual** — generative voice/text legacies; no video
  portrait; growing "deadbot" ethics criticism; consent posture unclear.
- **AI life-story interviewers (adjacent, capture-only):** Tell Mel (weekly
  phone calls), StoriedLife, Lifestoria, RememberWith, Memoirji — output is
  memoir text/audio, not an interactive twin. None uses a *visual talking
  avatar* as the interviewer.
- **Our position (verified then, stronger now):** the only consumer-priced
  end-to-end path from guided capture to a grounded generative twin with
  cloned voice + moving portrait. Retrieval products are safe-but-shallow;
  generative rivals are deep-but-risky; grounded-generative with enforced
  honesty is the defensible middle.

**Standing rule for our claims:** never "first AI legacy interviewer" (false);
the defensible sentence is "first consumer-priced, end-to-end living portrait:
guided capture → grounded twin → their voice and face."
